# Paycheck Sanity Checker

A small tool for reviewing pay stubs and flagging possible discrepancies before they become harder to unwind.

## Why It Exists

Hourly, tipped, and variable-schedule workers often have to trust payroll outputs they do not fully control. This prototype explores a practical workflow: collect the relevant pay-stub details, compare them against expected pay logic, and surface issues worth reviewing.

## What This Shows

- Translating a real operational frustration into a focused tool.
- Building a lightweight user-facing workflow around messy real-world inputs.
- Designing for nontechnical users who need clarity rather than dashboards for their own sake.
- Applying software thinking to hospitality/service-worker operations.
